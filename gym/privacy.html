النص الكامل للسياسة (بالإنجليزي)

In short: your gym's data stays on your own computer and your own door devices. If you turn on backups, copies go to your own Google Drive. Smart Zone receives no data of any kind and has no access to your files.

1.Who we are and what this covers

Z Gym is a Windows desktop application for running a gym or club, made by Smart Zone. This policy explains what the application accesses, where that data is kept, and what Smart Zone can and cannot see. A separate policy covers Z Shop.

2.Data the application stores

The gym's data — members (name, phone, e-mail, gender, optional photo, area and address, usual training days and hours), subscriptions, payments, attendance, guest visits, trainers and programs, employees with their salaries and leave, supplement sales and user passwords — is stored in a database file on the computer where the application is installed. By default this is the Windows user's local application data folder; the gym can choose another folder in Settings.

This data is never transmitted to Smart Zone.

3.Fingerprint data

If the gym uses fingerprint devices, members and staff enrol their fingerprint on the gym's own device. The application keeps an encrypted backup of the fingerprint template — a mathematical representation produced by the device, never a fingerprint image — in its local database, so that a replaced or additional door device can be set up again without re-scanning everyone.

Templates stay on the gym's computer and the gym's own devices. They are included in the gym's backups (still encrypted) and are never sent to Smart Zone.

4.Google Drive backup (optional)

Backing up to Google Drive is off by default. If you enable it and sign in, the application uses the Google Drive API under a single permission scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

This scope grants access only to files the application itself creates. The application cannot see, read, list or modify any other file in your Google Drive — not your documents, photos, or anything created by other applications.

What is uploaded
A copy of your gym's own database file
Where it goes
A folder named “Z Gym Backups” in your own Google Drive
Whose storage is used
Yours — it counts against your Google account's quota
Who can read it
Only you. Smart Zone has no access
Retention
You choose how many copies to keep; older ones are deleted automatically

Z Gym's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google account is used only to store and restore your backups, is never transferred to Smart Zone or anyone else, and is never used for advertising.

5.Your Google sign-in

When you link a Google account, Google issues the application an access token. That token is stored only on your computer, encrypted using the Windows Data Protection API and tied to your Windows user account. It is not transmitted anywhere else, and a copy of the file taken to another machine cannot be decrypted.

The application reads your account's email address solely to display which account is linked in its settings screen. It is not stored remotely or shared.

6.Network connections

The application connects to other systems only in these situations:

  • Door and fingerprint devices — ZKTeco and Hikvision devices that the gym adds in Settings, on the gym's own network. The application sends them each member's and employee's device ID and whether their access is valid, opens the door, and reads their attendance logs. If the gym turns on the network attendance receiver, the application also listens on the gym's local network for devices that report attendance on their own.
  • WhatsApp — the “send on WhatsApp” buttons open WhatsApp on the computer with a message filled in, which the staff member sends themselves. If the gym enters its own WhatsApp Business (Meta Cloud API) credentials, the application sends messages from the gym's own account through Meta: a welcome message with the member's name, code and QR card to new members, and alerts to the manager's number with a member's name, plan and amount. These messages are governed by Meta's terms and privacy policy.
  • Google Drive — only when you have enabled backups and linked an account, to upload or download your backup files.
  • Update checks — the application queries the public GitHub releases API to see whether a newer version exists. This request contains no gym data. As with any web request, GitHub sees the originating IP address.

There are no other outbound connections.

7.Analytics and tracking

None. The application contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services. No usage information is collected or transmitted.

8.Data sharing

Smart Zone receives no user data and therefore shares none — with anyone, for any purpose, including advertising, training machine-learning models, or sale to third parties.

When you use Google Drive backup, your use of Google's services is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

9.The gym's responsibility for member data

The gym decides what member and employee information to enter and is responsible for it, including telling members how their data and fingerprints are used and removing them when asked.

10.Your control

  • Disconnect at any time — the settings screen unlinks the Google account and removes the stored token from your computer.
  • Revoke access from Google — visit your Google account permissions and remove Z Gym.
  • Delete your backups — they are ordinary files in your own Drive. Delete them as you would any other file.
  • Delete local data — Settings → System → clear the database, or delete the application's data folder.

11.Children

Z Gym is business software intended for gym owners and their staff. It is not directed at children.
